1

Create an app in the Pcloud App Console and paste its App Key into the App Key box above, then tap Save.

2

About device storage
On device — what this phone holds: the library list, thumbnails, files not yet uploaded, and caches.

Caches — copies of files you opened lately so they open again instantly. No size limit, so they keep growing as you open files.
